How to Oil, Store & Maintain Your Ceremonial Talwar for Decades

📅 July 28, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read ✍️ Lineage Workshop

A ceremonial talwar is meant to outlast its owner — passing from father to son, displayed in the family haveli, or brought out for each generation's weddings. Proper maintenance ensures it remains pristine for decades. Here is our master craftsman's care ritual.

Monthly: The Oiling Ritual

Steel corrodes when exposed to air moisture. The single most important maintenance practice is regular oiling:

  • Use food-grade mineral oil or Renaissance Wax (museum-grade preservative)
  • Apply a thin, even coat using a clean, lint-free cloth
  • Work from base to tip in the direction of any pattern or grain
  • Wipe off excess oil — you want a micro-thin film, not a visible coating
  • Do not use WD-40 — it evaporates quickly and leaves residue
"Never touch the blade with bare hands — the oils and acids in human skin begin corroding high-carbon steel within hours."

Storage: Humidity is the Enemy

Ideal storage conditions:

  • Humidity: 45–55% relative humidity. Below 40% and wooden fittings can crack; above 60% and rust accelerates.
  • Temperature: 18–24°C. Avoid attics and basements where temperature swings are extreme.
  • Position: Store horizontally on a display rack, or vertically with tip down inside the scabbard.
  • Scabbard: Remove the blade from the velvet scabbard for long-term storage. Velvet can trap moisture against the blade.

Cleaning the Brass Hilt

Brass develops a natural patina (verdigris) over time — which many collectors prize. If you prefer the original bright finish:

  • Mix flour, salt, and white vinegar into a paste
  • Apply gently with a soft toothbrush, working into engravings
  • Rinse with warm water, dry immediately and completely
  • Apply a thin coat of Renaissance Wax to seal
  • Never use abrasive cleaners — they scratch the brass surface permanently

Velvet Scabbard Care

  • Spot-clean with a lint roller or soft brush — no water on velvet
  • Store in a cotton dust bag, never plastic (plastic traps moisture)
  • Keep away from direct sunlight — UV light fades velvet within months
  • Cedar blocks in the storage drawer repel moths and insects naturally

When to Seek Professional Re-Polishing

Surface rust spots (orange-brown discoloration) should be addressed immediately. For minor rust: rub gently with 0000-grade steel wool and mineral oil.

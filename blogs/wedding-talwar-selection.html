How to Choose the Perfect Wedding Talwar for Your Ceremony

📅 August 10, 2026 ⏱ 5 min read ✍️ Amritsar Master Forge

For centuries, the ceremonial talwar has been at the heart of Indian wedding processions — a symbol of royal lineage, protection, and masculine honor. Choosing the right one for your Anand Karaj or Baraat is a decision that will echo through your family's heritage for generations.

1. Understand the Purpose

Wedding talwars are crafted as ceremonial and display pieces — blunt, safe, and designed to be carried during the Baraat procession and photographed alongside traditional wedding attire. They are not functional weapons but heirloom artifacts.

"A groom carrying a talwar is not just carrying a sword — he is carrying the weight of every warrior ancestor who came before him."

2. Blade Curvature and Balance

Traditional Rajput talwars feature a distinctive curved single-edge blade with a pistol-grip hilt. For photographic purposes, you want a blade that photographs beautifully — high-polish carbon steel reflects light dramatically. Look for:

  • 28–32 inch blade length for proportional display
  • High-carbon steel with mirror polish for dramatic sheen
  • Slight curve (15–18°) for authentic Rajput silhouette
  • Balanced center of gravity 3–4 inches from the guard

3. Hilt Materials and Styles

The hilt is the most visible element in wedding photography. Our most popular choices:

Kundan Brass Hilt

Embedded with semi-precious stones in traditional Kundan setting. Gold-toned brass with intricate filigree work. The Jaipur Wedding Talwar features this hilt style — suitable for Sikh, Rajput, and Hindu ceremonies.

Gold-Plated Brass Hilt

A full gold-plated finish that catches light magnificently. The Shekhawati Bridal Talwar uses this style and pairs beautifully with ivory and white sherwani ensembles.

4. Scabbard Color Matching

Match your scabbard velvet to your wedding color scheme:

  • Royal Red Velvet — Classic, suits any sherwani color. Most traditional choice.
  • Deep Burgundy — Regal and modern. Pairs beautifully with dark navy or gold sherwanis.
  • White Zari Velvet — Perfect for cream or ivory outfits. Popular for destination weddings.
  • Midnight Blue — Contemporary choice for modern royal aesthetics.

5. Free Custom Laser Engraving

Every KraftMart wedding talwar comes with complimentary custom laser engraving on the blade spine. Popular inscription formats:

  • Bride & Groom names: "Gurpreet ❤️ Simran"
  • With wedding date: "Harman & Navjot · 24.10.2026"
  • Family motto in Punjabi or Sanskrit
  • Religious verse or Waheguru inscription

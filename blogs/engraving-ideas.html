50 Beautiful Blade Engraving Ideas for Your Wedding Sword

📅 July 15, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read ✍️ KraftMart Workshop

Free custom laser engraving is included with every KraftMart wedding talwar. The engraving is applied to the blade spine using precision CNC laser equipment — producing crisp, permanent inscriptions that will remain legible for generations. Here are our most popular and beautiful engraving ideas.

Classic Bride & Groom Combinations

  • Gurpreet ❤️ Simran · 24.10.2026
  • Rajveer Singh & Parveen Kaur — Forever
  • Harman ❤️ Navjot · Anand Karaj 2026
  • Vikram + Ananya · United in Love & Honor
  • Arjun & Deepika — 12 December 2026

Punjabi Inscriptions (Gurmukhi & Roman)

  • ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ (Waheguru) — Divine blessing
  • ਸਤ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ (Sat Sri Akal) — Timeless truth
  • Ik Onkar · Eternal Love of [Groom] & [Bride]
  • Chardi Kala — Ever-ascending spirit

Sanskrit & Hindi Phrases

  • सत्यमेव जयते (Satyameva Jayate) — Truth alone triumphs
  • धर्मो रक्षति रक्षितः — Righteousness protects those who protect it
  • रघुकुल रीति सदा चली आई — Raghukul tradition, eternally upheld
  • अहम् ब्रह्मास्मि — I am the Universe

Royal Family Mottos

  • "House of [Family Name] · Est. [Year]"
  • "In Service of God, Family & Honor"
  • "Protect. Provide. Prevail."
  • "The [Surname] Legacy — Forged in Punjab"
  • "By Blood and Steel, Forever Bound"

Religious & Spiritual Inscriptions

  • Om Namah Shivaya
  • Jai Mata Di
  • Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim
  • With God's Grace — [Names] — [Date]

Poetic & Literary Inscriptions

  • "Not the strength of the arm, but the resolve of the heart." — Rajput proverb
  • "The sword bends not — nor does our love." — Custom
  • "Forged in fire, tempered in love." — Custom
Pro tip: Engravings up to 60 characters display most clearly on a 28-inch blade. For longer texts, we recommend discussing layout with our workshop team via WhatsApp.

How to Submit Your Engraving Request

  1. Add your talwar to cart
  2. In the "Free Custom Engraving Notes" field in your cart, type your desired inscription exactly as you want it to appear
  3. Include any special characters, emojis, or Gurmukhi script by copy-pasting
  4. Our craftsman will contact you on WhatsApp for final approval before engraving

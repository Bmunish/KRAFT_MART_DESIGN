Lakshmi Engineering Works → KraftMart

In 1996, our foundational roots were laid in Amritsar under Lakshmi Engineering Works — mastering high-precision nickel and chrome electroplating, metal polishing, and custom vehicle metal fixtures. We developed an unrivalled mastery over steel alloys and brass metalworking.

Over three decades, that expertise evolved naturally into handforging authentic Indian ceremonial swords — Rajput talwars, Kirpans, and heritage artifacts. Today, KraftMart carries this legendary Amritsar lineage directly to wedding grooms and collectors worldwide via FedEx & DHL.

The Forging Process

From Raw Steel to Royal Blade

Every KraftMart talwar takes 7–10 skilled craftsman-hours to produce. Here's the journey.

🔥
Step 01

Forge & Shape

High-carbon or Damascus steel billets are heated and hammer-forged to the blade's final profile by our master smiths.

⚡
Step 02

Heat Treatment

Precision hardening and tempering brings Damascus blades to 56–58 HRC for the perfect balance of strength and flex.

✨
Step 03

Polish & Engrave

Hand-polishing brings the blade to mirror finish. Custom laser engraving of names and dates is then applied with precision.

📦
Step 04

Pack & Dispatch

Velvet-lined premium packaging. FedEx or DHL Express dispatch within 5–7 days to 42+ countries worldwide.

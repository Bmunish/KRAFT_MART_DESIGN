Chapter 01 · The Punjab Heritage

Situated in the Land of Five Rivers

Situated in the land of five rivers, We, Lakshmi Engineering Works, began our journey in 1996 as a product + service based enterprise.

The core of our service is Nickel and Chrome Electroplating and Polishing, making the goods ready to go for their active use. With rigorous precision and decades of chemical and metallurgical experience, we developed an unmatched reputation for producing mirror-lustre finishes, corrosion protection, and mechanical excellence.

Our Core Job-Work & Manufacturing Beginnings:
⚙️ Machinery & Corrugation Parts 🧵 Automated Stitching & Perforation Machines 🛠️ Hand-Tool Drilling Machines 🏍️ Vintage Vehicle Accessories (Silencer, Handles, Rims, etc.) ⚔️ Kirpan / Sword Blades – Majorly Our Job Work Service

From industrial heavy machinery components to the exacting contours of heritage vintage motorcycles and authentic steel blades, our 20+ years of high-calibre electroplating set the gold standard across Northern India.

Chapter 02 · Reverence & Sacred Art

Our Own Line of Products:
Sacred Sikh Prakash Articles

In addition to our industrial job work, we deal in sacred Sikh items, created specifically for Prakash purposes for Shri Guru Granth Sahib.

Being deeply associated with Sikhism and possessing the metallurgical mastery and "know-it-all science behind swords and blades," we poured our devotional craftsmanship into sacred ceremonial articles crafted with the utmost sanctity and aesthetic splendour.

🏹

🛡️

Prakash Chakar

✨

Prakash Chawar (Chaur Sahib)

Chapter 03 · Genesis & Global Triumph

Launching on 1st November, 2019 & Overcoming the Pandemic

With more than 20 years of experience in Nickel and Chrome plating, we decided to launch a dedicated product-based startup, embarking on its journey on 1st November, 2019.

🌱

In-House Manufacturing & City Roots

Having mastered all the metallurgical science behind swords, blades, and electro-chemical polishing, alongside a strong network and deep roots in our city and deep association with Sikhism, we started full in-house manufacturing for handicrafts and launched our products directly into the Indian market.

🌐

The Pandemic Pivot to Global E-Commerce

As the pandemic struck, physical operations were heavily affected. Instead of stopping, we rapidly explored digital avenues promoting worldwide artisanal commerce. We established our presence across trusted global marketplaces, connecting authentic Punjab craftsmanship with homes thousands of miles away.

Chapter 04 · The KraftMart Venture

A Venture Called KraftMart:
Your All-in-One Heritage Store

As a proud subsidiary of Lakshmi Engineering Works, at KraftMart, we specialize in creating exquisite Indo-traditional handicrafts, Sikh Kakar articles, and bidding accessories, catering to a diverse clientele who appreciate authentic and culturally rich items.

We take great pride in our exceptional craftsmanship, which is evident in every piece we produce — from intricately designed handicrafts to meaningful Sikh Kakar articles that hold deep spiritual significance. Additionally, we offer bespoke engraving services, allowing customers to personalize their purchases to their exact specifications, adding a unique personal touch to each heirloom.

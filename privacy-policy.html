This Privacy Policy describes how KraftMart (a subsidiary of Lakshmi Engineering Works, collectively "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit, use our services, or make a purchase from kraftmart.shop.

📋 Personal Information We Collect

To craft and deliver your personalized heritage pieces, we collect the following essential information:

  • Customer Details: Full name, billing address, shipping destination address, email address, and mobile phone number (for SMS/WhatsApp delivery notifications).
  • Bespoke Customization Data: Custom laser engraving names, wedding dates, family crest emblems, or Gurmukhi hymns submitted during checkout.
  • Payment Verification: Payment transaction confirmation IDs (note: complete credit card numbers and CVV codes are processed directly through secure PCI-DSS payment gateways and are never stored on our servers).
  • Customer Support Records: Order inquiries, sizing consultations, and unboxing verification communications.

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

  • Order Fulfillment: To forge, polish, assemble, laser engrave, and securely package your chosen articles.
  • Courier Transit: To generate shipping manifests and airway bills with courier partners (FedEx Express, DHL, Delhivery, DTDC, India Post).
  • Customer Support: To provide live order tracking, engraving mockups, and respond to your inquiries promptly.
  • Legal & Fraud Prevention: To screen transactions for unauthorized access and comply with domestic trade and export laws.
🔒 Security & Data Protection Commitment

We maintain strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or alteration of your personal data. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal records with third-party advertising brokers.

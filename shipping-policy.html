At Lakshmi Engineering Works, we ensure safe and timely delivery of our handcrafted products both within India and worldwide, using reliable shipping partners. Every item is inspected with master precision before dispatch from our Amritsar workshop.

🚚 Domestic Shipping (India)

  • We deliver pan-India through verified premier courier partners including Delhivery, DTDC, and India Post.
  • Estimated Delivery Time: 8–10 business days (after processing & crafting time).
  • Delivery timelines may vary slightly depending on your exact postal code and local courier service operations.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) is available with 15% booking advance + 85% payable upon parcel receipt at your doorstep.

🌍 International Shipping (Exports)

  • For our global customers, we ship using trusted international couriers such as India Post International, FedEx, and DHL.
  • Estimated Transit/Delivery Time: 7–8 business days (after processing time; guaranteed delivery within 10 days excluding processing).
  • Destinations served include: United States of America, United Kingdom, Canada, United Arab Emirates, Europe, Australia, and 40+ countries worldwide.
  • Actual transit time may vary slightly based on destination country customs clearance and courier flight logistics.
🔧 Processing Time (Before Dispatch)

Please note that processing time varies by product, depending on the level of craftsmanship, steel blade forging, brass casting, and custom laser/diamond engraving requested. The estimated processing time for each specific item is clearly mentioned on its product page.

📦 Tracking & Delivery Support

Once your order is handed over to the courier partner, an automatic shipping confirmation containing your FedEx / Delhivery / India Post Tracking Number and direct tracking URL will be sent to your registered email and WhatsApp number.

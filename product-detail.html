⚔️ Is It Real or Plastic? ▾

100% Authentic Metal & Organic Materials — Zero Plastic:

At KraftMart, we strictly forbid plastic or flimsy imitation molds. Every sword, dagger, and kada is forged from authentic solid metals:

  • Blade / Metal Structure: Genuine High-Carbon Steel, Forged Sarabloh (Pure Iron), or Layered Damascus Steel with authentic ceremonial heft (~1.1 to 1.3 kg for swords, ~190g for Sarabloh kadas).
  • Hilt & Mounts: Solid cast brass, forged steel, or heavy nickel-brass electroplated with gold/antique patina.
  • Scabbard: Carved seasoned wood core wrapped in pure velvet, embroidered zari, or genuine leather.
  • Ceremonial Edge (Legal & Safe): The blades are crafted with ceremonial unsharpened (dull) safety edges to comply strictly with Indian courier regulations and wedding ceremonial safety.

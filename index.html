OUR LEGACY SINCE 1996

The Amritsar Craftsmanship Story

Starting in 1996 as Lakshmi Engineering Works in Amritsar, Punjab, our journey began with precision nickel and chrome electroplating, metal polishing, and vehicle accessories. Over three decades, that master metalworking expertise evolved into handforging authentic Indian ceremonial swords, Kirpans, and heritage artifacts.

Today, KraftMart carries forward Amritsar's legendary metalworking lineage directly to wedding couples and weapon collectors across the globe via FedEx & DHL, complete with bespoke name and date engraving.

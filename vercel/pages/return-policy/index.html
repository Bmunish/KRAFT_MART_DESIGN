At Lakshmi Engineering Works, we take great pride in our handcrafted products. However, due to the customized nature of our swords, daggers, and handicrafts, we do not accept returns or refunds for international orders.

If you face any issue with your order, we encourage you to reach out to us, and we will work together to resolve your concerns amicably.

🚫 Return & Refund Terms

🌏 For International Orders (Exports):

  • No returns or refunds are accepted once exported due to strict international cross-border logistics and customs regulations.
  • In exceptional cases where a return is agreed upon after mutual discussion with our management, the buyer must bear all return shipping costs, customs duties, and taxes.

🇮🇳 For Domestic (India) Orders:

  • Returns are allowed only for defective, damaged, or incorrect items delivered.
  • In such cases, the buyer must share clear unboxing proof, and the return request must be pre-approved by our support team.
  • All return requests must be raised within the valid 3-day window from receipt.

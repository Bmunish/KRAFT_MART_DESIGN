SITUATE IN THE LAND OF FIVE RIVERS

Our 1996 Roots: Electroplating, Polishing & Blade Craft

Situate in the land of five rivers, We, Lakshmi Engineering Works, began our journey in 1996 as a product + service based enterprise. The core of our service is Nickel and Chrome Electroplating and Polishing, making the goods ready to go for their use.

Main products started from & currently doing for job work:
  • ❖ Machinery and Corrugation parts.
  • ❖ Automated Stitching And Perforation machines.
  • ❖ Hand-tool drilling machines.
  • ❖ Accessories of various vintage vehicles (silencer, handles, rims, etc.)
  • ⚔️ Kirpan / Sword blades – MAJORLY OUR JOB WORK SERVICE.

Our Own Line of Products: In addition to this, we also deal in Sikh Items used for Prakash purposes for Shri Guru Granth Sahib (Prakash Arrowhead / Teer, Prakash Chakar, Prakash Chawar). Backed by 20+ years of electroplating mastery and deep association with Sikhism, we embarked on a product-based startup on 1st November, 2019. Serving 4,900+ clients worldwide.

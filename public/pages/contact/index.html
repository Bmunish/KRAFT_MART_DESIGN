Common Questions

🪙
How does Cash on Delivery work?

Pay 15% advance to confirm your custom order, then 85% when the sword arrives at your doorstep. Full advance orders get an extra 5% discount.

✍
How do I request custom laser engraving?

Simply mention the text you want (names, dates, shlokas) in your order notes or WhatsApp us. Engraving is FREE on all orders — our craftsmen apply it before final polishing.

✈
Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We dispatch worldwide to USA, Canada, UK, Europe, Australia, UAE and beyond via FedEx and DHL Express. Full tracking details emailed upon dispatch.

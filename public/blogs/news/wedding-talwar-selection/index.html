How to Choose the Perfect Wedding Talwar

Authored by Amritsar Master Forge · Lineage Workshop

In Indian weddings, the ceremonial sword carried by the groom is far more than an accessory — it is a sacred emblem of protection, honor, and enduring family lineage.

1. Blade Alloy: Choosing Between Damascus and Carbon Steel

When selecting a groom talwar, the choice of steel defines both the weight balance and the aesthetic prestige of the blade:

  • 1095/15N20 Damascus Steel: Forged from over 300 folds of high-carbon steel, creating iridescent wood-grain swirls that catch the light during photography. Rated 56–58 HRC.
  • Mirror-Polished Carbon Steel: Authentic mirror chrome luster with superior flex resistance, ideal for traditional Anand Karaj and Baraat ceremonies.

2. Scabbard Velvet Matching with Royal Sherwani

Our master scabbards are carved from seasoned rosewood and wrapped in heavy bridal velvet. For deep crimson or maroon sherwanis, the classic Royal Red Velvet sheath is universally favored. For ivory or gold sherwanis, emerald green or midnight navy velvet creates a striking royal contrast.

3. Complimentary Custom Laser Inscription

KraftMart provides free fiber-laser engraving along the spine of the blade. Grooms frequently request Bride & Groom names, the wedding date, or sacred blessings in Gurmukhi, Devanagari, or English calligraphy.
